# Multi-omics Profiling of the Lateral Ventricle Choroid Plexus Reveals Developmental  Cellular Remodeling, Early Immune Gene Activation, and a Novel Epithelial Subtype

**DOI:**

# Abstract
Brain development and function depend on the choroid plexus, whose dynamic cellular and transcriptional changes shape immune cell trafficking and cerebrospinal fluid composition. Here, we performed a comprehensive multi-omics analysis—including bulk and single-cell transcriptomics and epigenomics—of the lateral ventricle choroid plexus across postnatal and adult stages in mice and rats. We uncovered changes in the choroid plexus cellular composition and gene expression profiles, including an early and progressive immune gene activation in all main cell types from early postnatal to young adult stage. Epithelial cells exhibited plasticity over time, characterized by distinct gene expression programs, locus-specific changes in chromatin accessibility, and subtype diversity including a newly identified cell subtype with unique gene markers. Our study offers a comprehensive atlas of transcriptional activity and chromatin accessibility in choroid plexus cells, providing a valuable resource to guide future research.
